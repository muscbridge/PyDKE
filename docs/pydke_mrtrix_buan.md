# Experimental pyDKE → MRtrix → DIPY BUAN bridge

This feature is a research prototype, not a validated clinical or group-analysis pipeline. It operates on a participant's existing pyDKE diffusion ODF SH, mask and scalar maps, tracks in native space with MRtrix, aligns to a streamline atlas with DIPY SLR, recognizes bundles with RecoBundles, and assigns native scalar samples to BUAN centroid disks. Source files are read only.

## Critical basis and orientation distinction

The current `pydesigner.tractography.odf.shbasis(..., "tournier")` differs by coefficient signs from DIPY's non-legacy Tournier/MRtrix3 basis. `pydke_to_mrtrix` implements the sign mapping and tests equality of evaluated functions at sampled directions. This is NOT the DIPY legacy-Descoteaux/MRtrix coefficient permutation. The pipeline additionally derives a frame transform by matching the pyDKE input b-vectors to the scanner-space gradients exported by MRtrix, rejects inconsistent tables, and rotates or reflects the SH accordingly. Historical outputs may have been generated by another version. The export path may also apply an axis flip; NIfTI affine, image storage, SH coordinate frame, gradient/tensor conventions, and all prior flips require independent physical-direction validation. Basis function equality alone does not establish anatomical correctness. The pipeline will only track when the operator explicitly attests `--coordinate-provenance-verified`. This switch is not evidence of validation. Validate against known-direction synthetic tensors and real anatomy, and establish the producing commit before use.

DKI diffusion ODFs are not CSD fiber ODFs. Their amplitude and angular behavior differ, can be negative, and `iFOD2` thresholds are not transferable from CSD. Determine cutoff and any rectification through a documented sensitivity study. The `--cutoff` option is deliberately mandatory; 0.1 is not an endorsed default. A finite/extreme-value gate (>1000) is a coarse guard, not a scientific quality certificate. Mask and all scalar inputs must share the native SH image grid. `--exclude-extreme-voxels` creates a derived tracking and sampling mask excluding voxels with any coefficient above 1000 in magnitude, and zeros those voxels in the output SH. It is an exploratory triage rule and does not repair neighboring voxels or establish validity. Inspect negative amplitudes and tractography anatomy. This module does not rectify ODFs automatically; see the separately provided `rectify-odf` command and validate its inputs and consequences before any use.

## Running

Use a pyDKE environment with compatible DIPY and MRtrix3 commands on PATH. Provide an atlas whole-brain tractogram and named atlas bundles. Preflight first:

```
pydke-buan --odf dki_odf.nii --mask brain_mask.nii --reference B0.nii \
  --bvec dwi_preprocessed.bvec --gradient-mif dwi_preprocessed.mif \
  --metric dti_fa.nii --metric dki_mk.nii \
  --atlas-wholebrain whole_brain_MNI.trk --atlas-bundle CST_L.trk \
  --basis pydke-tournier --cutoff 0.1 --out pilot_preflight --preflight-only
```

After independent coordinate and provenance validation, use a **new output directory**, add `--coordinate-provenance-verified`, and set a justified `--cutoff`, `--count` and `--threads`. Non-deterministic tracking and chosen parameters must be accounted for in interpretation. Output includes input hashes, stage logs, transformed SH, tractograms and point-level disk assignments with separate simple unweighted disk means. These summaries are for QC, not the paper's linear mixed models or its inferential pipeline. Missing points remain missing; this is not an automatic complete BUAN statistical analysis. Nonlinear image warps are not used here. Check spatial overlays, streamline order, bundle orientation, anatomical plausibility, sampling support, and model-bundle coverage; inspect registration and extraction for each participant and bundle. Failures or incomplete bundles must not be presented as negative findings.

The Figshare 30-bundle atlas cited by DIPY is external data, not part of this repository: https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.12089652.v5. Preserve its license and citations. BUAN: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-74054-4.

The matching gradient inputs must represent the exact same ordered volumes and processing stage; the pyDKE fit must have used the supplied b-vectors without an undocumented sign modification. The gradient match is an internal consistency check, not a substitute for inspecting anatomical orientation.

## Pilot observations

A technical pilot confirmed the sign conversion and gradient-frame mapping using synthetic directions and MRtrix peak extraction, and compared corrected peaks with independent CSD directions. Direct iFOD2 tracking on diffusion ODFs at a provisional cutoff generated implausibly long, poorly registered tracks and did not reliably recognize the tested bundles. A CSD-derived tractogram allowed recognition and disk sampling as a positive control. These results do not validate a cutoff or the diffusion-ODF tractography route. Successful command execution or a nonempty bundle does not imply anatomical validity; review quality before use.

## Tracking sensitivity and pilot configuration

For research comparisons the bridge accepts `--tracking-mask`, `--algorithm`, `--tracking-power`, `--seed-limit`, `--rng-seed`, `--step-mm`, `--angle-deg`, `--slr-x0` and `--slr-min-cluster`; the optional tracking mask is intersected with the finite/extreme-coefficient QC mask and must match its native grid. A tested exploratory configuration used a derived FA >= 0.2 mask, `--algorithm SD_STREAM --cutoff 0.05 --step-mm 1.25 --angle-deg 45 --slr-x0 rigid --slr-min-cluster 3` and 5,000 tracks. This produced recognizable examples in three participants, but the threshold and parameters are not validated defaults. Bundle selection, mask effects on coverage, negative ODF amplitudes, altered scaling, random seeding, and local recognition registration can all affect results. The output uses point-wise unweighted means for QA; this is not the BUAN group mixed-effects inference. Preserve masks, settings, seeds/versions and QC; compare with an independent tracking control and inspect original-space anatomy before research interpretation.

`--rng-seed` passes `MRTRIX_RNG_SEED` to MRtrix and is recorded; multi-thread scheduling can still change results. The FA-derived mask may bias subsequent FA sampling and must not be treated as neutral across participants. Rectification is a separate optional sensitivity analysis, not automatic in this bridge.

For historically generated ODF files with an unknown producer version, `--experimental-sampled-check` can supply a JSON audit generated by an external cohort validation workflow. It checks the ODF, b-vector and gradient-MIF hashes and accepts only a passing record. Such checks provide limited, sampled evidence of export and orientation consistency; they do not establish the historical software version, whole-brain validity or accurate crossing-fiber structure. The operator is responsible for defining and documenting the audit and for inspecting all flags.
